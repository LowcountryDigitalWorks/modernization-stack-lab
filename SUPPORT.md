# Support and maintenance

Use repository issues for synthetic lab defects and proof requests. Do not include customer
data or credentials. Owning Portfolio approves provider proof sequence and acceptance;
provider systems and component repositories retain their own operational authority.

Security reports follow SECURITY.md and the organization security contact.
Foundation acceptance is LAB-001, not a product launch/release. Maintenance uses reviewed PRs,
the lockfile and deterministic checks. No standing automation, service SLA or customer
support obligation is created by this repository.
